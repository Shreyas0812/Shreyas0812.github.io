Controls · Multi-robot

SwarmSync

Distributed MPC for multi-drone motion planning with goals that move mid-flight. Extends the online DMPC framework of Luis et al. (2020) with an online Hungarian-algorithm reallocation layer that re-assigns drones to goals during execution. Achieves 100% goal success and zero collisions across all test scenarios, with the QP solver running above 200 Hz (real-time). Tested up to 64 agents.

100% success · 0 collisions Up to 64 agents C++ solver · Python viz

Motion planning

SBAMP

Sampling-based adaptive motion planner integrating RRT* with a Lyapunov-stable SEDS-inspired controller for real-time goal tracking. Replans fast enough to track moving goals at speeds where the static RRT* baseline fails; hardware-validated on RoboRacer. Published at the ICRA 2026 Workshop on Frontiers of Optimization for Robotics ↗.

Multi-agent · Thesis

LCBA Warehouse System

Decentralized task allocation for warehouse automation. LCBA extends GCBBA with local subgraph consensus and event-driven replanning that stays functional even when communication graphs become disconnected, where CBBA and SGA collapse. Sustains ~0.80 tasks/timestep (near capacity) with a 6× throughput gap over CBBA — and maintains non-zero task completion in scenarios where CBBA and SGA drop to zero. Integrates Cooperative A* and RHCR planners.

6× throughput vs prior state-of-art Up to 470 agents · 432 runs Python

Manipulation · Hardware

Franka Emika Pick & Place

Full pick-and-place and block-stacking system on the 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda arm. Used damped least-squares / pseudo-inverse IK with null-space optimization and pre-computed emergency fallback configs, plus RRT and potential-field planners for collision-free paths; AprilTag detection with a complementary filter gave stable pose estimation, and predictive timing handled dynamic block interception on a rotating turntable. Placed 3rd in the class competition.

Perception · State Estimation

Quaternion-based UKF

Unscented Kalman Filter for drift-free 3D orientation estimation from a 3-axis gyroscope and accelerometer. Propagates sigma points on the quaternion manifold to maintain valid rotations throughout the filter; validated roll, pitch, and yaw against Vicon motion-capture ground truth.

Controls · Reinforcement Learning

PPO Drone Hover

PPO implemented from scratch for quadrotor control in gym-pybullet-drones — full algorithm including GAE, clipped surrogate objective, and entropy regularisation, with every code block mapped to the course math. Policy converged cleanly: reward ~1.0 → ~1.9, explained variance ~0.83, clip fraction 0.03–0.09 across 4M environment steps trained on CPU (~2.5 h). The drone lifts consistently toward the 1.0 m target; stable hover is a reward shaping gap (upward-velocity bonus drove fast ascent over altitude stability) — algorithmic correctness is confirmed.
